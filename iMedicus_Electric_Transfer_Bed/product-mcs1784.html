MCS1784 Electrosurgical Generator

with Bipolar Vessel Sealing

iMedicus · MCS1784

Overview

A universal electrosurgical generator for cutting, coagulation and vessel sealing, for departments that demand precise output control and high energy output.

300 Wmax cutting power
10user presets
3outputs
CQMreturn-pad safety
  • Cut: Pure, Blend 1, Blend 2, Blend 3 and Endo Cut
  • Coag: Spray, Forced and Soft
  • Bipolar: Macro, Standard and Precise
  • Macro bipolar for vessel sealing (LigaSure-type instruments)
  • Endo Cut for polypectomy and papillotomy
  • Two monopolar and one bipolar output
  • 10 user presets for fast procedure set-up
  • CQM contact monitoring, return-electrode and equipment-failure alarms
