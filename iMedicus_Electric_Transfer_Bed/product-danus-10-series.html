Danus 10 Infinity

Ultimate Mobility, Superb Performance

iMedicus · Danus 10 Series

Overview

Mobile freedom with a seamless workflow. The Danus 10 is built for efficient, versatile diagnostics, especially general imaging, point-of-care and OB/GYN, and folds to under 80 cm for transport between sites.

21.5"HD monitor
13.3"touch screen
<80 cmfolded height
3 h+battery (optional)
  • Foldable design under 80 cm, transportable by SUV or MPV
  • 4 oblique front transducer ports for easy plugging and unplugging
  • DFS damping folding system protects the monitor arm and screen
  • One-key electromagnetic height adjustment
  • fCharge wireless charging and fingerprint multi-account login
  • iQ Suite: Auto EF, Auto OB, Auto Simpson, Auto IMT, Auto NT, Auto Volume Flow
  • fDepth 3D/4D rendering, fAssist scanning tutorials and fIntelligent image styles
  • eBiopsy+ needle guidance with auto steering; ProShake™, remote-control probes and voice control
